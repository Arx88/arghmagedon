Kenney's original license notices are preserved in `licenses/`.

Music and ambience were encoded as 44.1 kHz Vorbis, with measured loudness targets of −20 LUFS (music) and −24 LUFS (ambience). End/start crossfades make exploration and environmental samples loop without an abrupt splice. Pirate Indenture keeps its authored loop. Original foley remains unchanged; gain envelopes, layering, small pitch variation and spatial mixing occur at runtime. The published wind and winch samples were synthesized/arranged by their original authors; this game does not synthesize its effects from oscillators.

`manifest.json` records every runtime file's author, source, license, original download/member, SHA-256, codec, duration and processing. The runtime set contains 63 recordings and tracks, approximately 5.13 MB. Unused original archives are not shipped.
